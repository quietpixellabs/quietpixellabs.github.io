How it works

From long page to one file
in three quiet steps.

  1. 01

    Open the page

    Go to the webpage you want to keep. PageStitch stays out of sight until you need it.

  2. 02

    Start the capture

    Choose PageStitch from your browser. It works through the page from top to bottom.

  3. 03

    Save the result

    Download one complete image, ready to archive, annotate, or share.

Private by design

Your pages stay yours.

PageStitch does its work on your device. We do not collect page contents, screenshots, or browsing history.
